Just Keep Swimming

A rescue robot is lost in a flooded building. Grover's search answers two questions: Where am I? and How do I get home?

Click a cell to drop the robot.

Stage 1 · Where am I?

Drop the robot, then press Sense. The robot only feels which sides of its cell have walls.

Stage 2 · How do I get home?

After sensing, press Go home. Grover searches all 64 three-move plans for one that reaches HOME from every place the robot might be.
